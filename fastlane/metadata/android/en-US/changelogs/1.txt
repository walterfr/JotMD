Initial release 0.1.0:
- Native block-based Markdown reader and editor in Jetpack Compose (no WebView)
- SAF file and folder access with autosave
- Multi-tab support, [[wikilinks]], #tags, and folder FTS4 full-text search
- In-note find/replace, YAML front matter, GFM tables, tasks, alerts, and ==highlight==
- Light/dark themes, source mode, and HTML/PDF export
